# Olist Store Analysis E-Commerce

# 📌 Project Overview
Olist Store Analysis is an e-commerce data analytics project that examines sales, customers, products, payments, reviews, and delivery performance. Using SQL and visualization tools, it identifies business trends, customer behavior, and key insights to support data-driven decision-making and improve growth.

# 📊 Key Insights
- Identified peak sales periods and seasonal trends.
- Weekday orders generated more revenue than weekends.
- Credit card was the most preferred payment method.
- Higher-rated products and sellers received more repeat purchases.
- Delivery delays negatively impacted customer review scores.
- Certain product categories contributed the highest revenue.
- Major cities and states accounted for a large share of orders.
- Customers with positive reviews showed higher purchasing activity.
- Fast delivery was strongly associated with better customer satisfaction.
- Revenue and order volume varied significantly across regions and product categories.
